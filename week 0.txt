1. The geographical area within which a species can naturally be found is its:
(A) Tolerance
(B) Abundance
(C) Range
(D) Affluence
Correct Answer: (C) Range

2. Excess nitrogen fertilizer enters a pond, causes algal growth, and later creates oxygen-poor conditions. This process shows disturbance in:
(A) Political geography
(B) Biogeochemical cycles
(C) Cartographic methods
(D) Habitat selection
Correct Answer: (B) Biogeochemical cycles

3. The discipline of Demography is most closely related to:
(A) Phytogeography
(B) Zoogeography
(C) Population Geography
(D) Economic Geography
Correct Answer: (C) Population Geography

4. A dam traps sediment upstream, reducing the amount of silt reaching a delta. The likely result is:
(A) Expansion of the ozone layer
(B) Increase in coral shell formation
(C) Change in the geomorphology of the delta
(D) Increase in ultraviolet radiation
Correct Answer: (C) Change in the geomorphology of the delta

5. The quantum of human impacts can be written as:
(A) I = P + A + T
(B) I = P X A + T
(C) I = P X A X T
(D) I = P + A X T
Correct Answer: (C) I = P X A X T

6. A large continuous forest is split by a highway into two smaller patches, each too small to support wide-ranging animals. This is:
(A) Habitat degradation
(B) Habitat displacement
(C) Habitat loss
(D) Habitat fragmentation
Correct Answer: (D) Habitat fragmentation

7. Increased atmospheric carbon dioxide dissolves into oceans and makes survival difficult for organisms with calcium carbonate shells. This is mainly due to:
(A) Nitrogen fixation
(B) Ozone formation
(C) Ocean acidification
(D) Soil waterlogging
Correct Answer: (C) Ocean acidification

8. The Trinity explosion of 1945 is taken as the beginning of the:
(A) Holocene
(B) Cenocene
(C) Anthropocene
(D) Eocene
Correct Answer: (C) Anthropocene

9. The movement of individuals away from their place of birth or hatching or seed production into a new habitat or area to survive and reproduce is called:
(A) translocation
(B) migration
(C) dispersal
(D) drifting
Correct Answer: (C) dispersal

10. Sustainable harvest of resources falls under the category of:
(A) conservation
(B) preservation
(C) environmentalism
(D) none of the above
Correct Answer: (A) conservation
